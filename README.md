# Dice Game – Two Player
A fun and interactive two-player dice game built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
Players take turns rolling a dice, and the first to reach 100 points wins!

##  Game Features

- Two players playing turn-by-turn on the same screen.
- On each turn, a player rolls the dice:
- If the player rolls **any number except 0**, it adds to their current score.
- If the player rolls a **0**, their **current score resets to 0** and the turn passes to the other player.
- Players can choose to **“Hold”** to save their current score to the total and end their turn.
- The first player to reach **100 points** is declared the winner.

## Rules Summary

- Rolling a **0** cancels the round score and ends your turn.
- Use **“Hold”** to bank your points before taking a risk!
- Game ends when a player reaches **100 total points**.
